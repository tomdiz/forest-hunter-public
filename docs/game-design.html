Forest Hunter

A hunter with a revolver follows a river through a pine forest, past a log cabin and its campfire, hunting the evil stag with glowing red eyes that hides around the map. Foxes, deer and rabbits wander the valley and run when the hunter gets close. The stag peeks out, stalks the hunter, charges, and retreats to a new hiding spot. Shoot it five times to win. Lose all three hearts and it's over.

Built as a first Godot project in Xogot, in GDScript, with free assets. This page describes the game as it is built now. The project is in a private GitHub repo.

Where it stands

The core loop works from start to win or lose. A few things in the original plan are not built yet.

Working

  • Isometric camera that follows the hunter
  • WASD or arrow movement, with run animations
  • Mouse aiming and a hitscan revolver with tracer and flash
  • Touch controls: two thumb sticks and a reload button
  • Three plank bridges to the far bank (one near the start), where some of the stag's hiding spots are
  • Painted revolver held in the right hand, always pointing the way the hunter faces (a pistol and the original rifle are also in the code)
  • Title screen over the live forest, shown once per run, with a camera glide into the game
  • Options menu with separate Music and Effects volume sliders, remembered between runs
  • Background music: a calm loop that crossfades to a tense one while the stag hunts you, and fades out for the end screens
  • Sound effects: gunshot, reload, footsteps, stag, animals, pickups, campfire, forest ambience and win and lose stings
  • Log cabin with a porch, glowing window, chimney smoke and a furnished room you can walk into
  • Usable cabin props: a door that opens and shuts, a bed that restores your health once, a table with cartridges and a fireplace you can put out
  • Pine-green trees with soft shadows, lit by a warm sun and cool ambient light
  • Ammo, reload and an ammo box at the cabin
  • River, forest, rocks, cabin and campfire
  • Wandering foxes, deer and rabbits that flee and can be shot
  • The stag: hide, peek, stalk, charge, retreat, die
  • Stag finds its way with a navigation mesh
  • HUD with hearts, ammo and stag health
  • Win and lose screens with Play again

Not built or rough

  • Rabbits are a static model that hops in code, so they have no real animation
  • Animals only wander near where they start and do not react to gunshots
  • Touch controls are tested with simulated touches on the Mac only, not on a real iPhone
  • The revolver is held in one hand and swings with the arm. No recoil or reload animation
  • The cabin is made from simple shapes (logs are cylinders), so it is plain inside
  • A mute button for touch screens (M mutes the music on a keyboard)
  • The sound effects and music are simple synthesised sounds. They are good enough for a demo, but recorded audio would sound more natural
  • Only 24 of the planned 55 trees fit inside the walkable valley

The world

A Diablo-style walk: the river is the path, a valley about 22 m wide follows it, and dense pines wall in the sides. The map below is drawn from the real object positions in the level (plan view, about 83 m from west to east).

Hunter start / campfire Stag hiding spot (red eyes) River (cross it on a bridge) Walkable valley Pine forest Rocks Plank bridge Ammo box Fox Deer Rabbit

The river blocks movement except at three plank bridges, one near the start, which the stag uses too. Past each end wall the water carries on (about 34 m) so it never stops in view. Invisible walls 11 m from the river keep the hunter in the valley. The level has 230 pines behind the walls, 24 inside the valley with solid trunks, 23 rocks along the banks, bushes, grass, stumps and logs.

Controls and movement

Move and aim are separate: you can run one way while facing another. The run animation changes to match (forward, backward or side step). Touch controls feed the same input actions as the keyboard, so movement works the same on both.

Mac

  • WASD / arrows: move, relative to the screen
  • Mouse: aim. The hunter turns to face the cursor
  • Left click or Space: fire. Hold to keep firing
  • R: reload
  • E: use what you are standing next to (a prompt appears at the bottom of the screen)
  • M: mute or unmute the music
  • Esc or the Options button: open the volume menu (pauses the game)
  • Enter or click Play: start from the title screen, and Play again on the end screen

iPhone (touch)

  • Left half of the screen: a floating stick that moves the hunter
  • Right half: a floating stick that aims. Push it out past 40% and the revolver fires, and the knob turns orange
  • R button, bottom right: reload
  • E button, above R: appears when something is in reach
  • Options, top right: open the volume menu
  • Tap anywhere on the title screen to start, and on the end screen to play again
  • The sticks appear only when the device has a touch screen. To see them on a Mac, turn on always_show on the touch overlay

Isometric camera

  • Orthogonal Camera3D, turned 45° and tilted 35° down, about 10 m of the level visible across
  • Rides on a rig that follows the hunter with a short, smooth lag
  • Input is turned 45° so "up" goes up the screen

Movement and aiming, in GDScript

func _physics_process(delta: float) -> void:
	var input := Input.get_vector("move_left", "move_right", "move_up", "move_down")
	var dir := Vector3(input.x, 0.0, input.y).rotated(Vector3.UP, deg_to_rad(CAMERA_YAW))
	velocity.x = dir.x * SPEED          # SPEED = 5.0 metres per second
	velocity.z = dir.z * SPEED
	move_and_slide()

	# Face the mouse: a ray from the camera through the cursor, cut at chest height.
	var aim := _aim_direction()
	if aim != Vector3.ZERO:
		model.rotation.y = lerp_angle(model.rotation.y, atan2(aim.x, aim.z), TURN_SPEED * delta)

Game logic

Five parts: the hunter, the shot, the ammo, the wandering animals and the stag. All numbers below are the values in the scripts, so they are easy to tune.

Hunter

  • 3 hearts, walks at 5 m/s
  • Blocked by the river, the valley walls, rocks and tree trunks
  • At 0 hearts he falls and the game-over screen follows

Revolver

  • Hitscan: one ray from the muzzle, 40 m long, 4 shots a second
  • A short yellow tracer and an orange flash of light on each shot
  • The first thing the ray hits takes the damage, if it has a take_damage method. That includes the animals

Wandering animals

  • 3 foxes, 3 deer and 8 rabbits (in small groups), placed along the valley
  • Each strolls to random spots within a few metres of where it started, pauses, and walks on. Animated ones play Walk, Idle or Eating
  • Within 6 to 9 m of the hunter they run (Gallop, or faster hops), and calm down again about 4 m beyond that
  • One shot kills them. Foxes and deer play Death, then vanish. Rabbits just vanish

Ammo

  • 6 rounds in the revolver (one per chamber), 24 in reserve
  • R reloads in 1.5 s, and it reloads by itself when empty. No shooting while reloading
  • An ammo box by the cabin door gives +12 once

Hunter animations

  • Standing: Idle_Gun_Pointing
  • Moving: Run, Run_Back, Run_Left or Run_Right, chosen by the angle between walking and facing: within 60° is forward, beyond 120° is backward, the rest is a side step
  • Dying: Death

The stag

It has 5 hit points and hides at one of 5 spots. Its glowing red eyes show through the trees, so the player has a hint of where to look before it moves.

Stag animations for each state

  • Hidden: Idle_Headlow
  • Peek: Idle, facing the hunter
  • Stalk: Walk at 2 m/s
  • Charge: Gallop at 7 m/s. A touch costs the hunter one heart
  • Retreat: Gallop to another spot
  • Hit by a shot: a half-second stun with Idle_HitReact1 or 2
  • Dead: Death, and the eyes go out

How it gets around

The level has a baked navigation mesh, built from the colliders (river, walls, rocks, trunks), and the stag follows it with a NavigationAgent3D. It walks around obstacles instead of through them.

Inside the cabin

Walk up to a prop and a prompt shows at the bottom of the screen. Press E (or tap the E button) to use it.

Door

Opens and shuts. A shut door blocks the hunter, the stag and the animals, and the stag gives up charging at it after 6 seconds and retreats. The door will not shut on you while you stand in the doorway, and the stag can still hit you from just outside if you stand next to it.

Bed

Sleep for a moment (the screen fades to black) to get all your hearts back. It works once, and only if you are hurt.

Table

Take a box of cartridges for 6 more rounds. Once only.

Fireplace

Put the fire out or light it again. It only changes the light and the glow of the embers.

Win

The stag's 5 hit points reach 0. After 1.6 s a gold "YOU GOT IT" screen appears with a Play again button.

Lose

The hunter's 3 hearts are gone. After 1.6 s a red "GAME OVER" screen appears with the same button. Play again reloads the level.

HUD

Hearts top left, stag health bar top center, ammo bottom left with "RELOADING" while it reloads.
